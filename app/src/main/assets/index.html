How to play

One player holds the phone against their forehead, screen facing their friends. Friends describe, act out, or make sounds for the word on screen — without saying the word itself (or any part of it!). When the guesser gets it, tilt the phone down for CORRECT. Stuck? Tilt up to PASS. No tilt sensor? No problem — the big on-screen buttons and the ↓ / ↑ arrow keys work exactly the same. You can flip which tilt means what in Settings below.
