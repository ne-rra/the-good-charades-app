# How to Play — The Good Charades App

## The setup
1. Pick a deck (or **Mixed Bag** for everything).
2. Choose your round length in Settings: 30, 60, 90, or 120 seconds.
3. Tap a deck, then **Start round**. After the 3-2-1 countdown, hold the phone against your forehead with the screen facing your friends.

## The rules
- Your friends describe, act out, hum, or make sounds for the word on screen.
- They **cannot say the word itself** — or any part of it — and no rhyming hints ("sounds like…").
- When you guess it: **tilt the phone DOWN** = ✅ Got it.
- Stuck? **Tilt the phone UP** = ⏭️ Pass and move on.
- The big **GOT IT!** / **PASS** buttons work too. Tapping the word itself also counts — but only ever as **GOT IT!**, never as a pass.
- 🔥 **Streak bonus:** every 5 correct answers in a row adds **+3 seconds** to the clock.
- ⭐ Your best score on each deck is remembered and shown on its card.

## Settings worth knowing
- **Flip tilt** — the classic layout is down = correct, up = pass. If your group plays it the other way, flip it in Settings.
- **Sound effects & vibration** — on by default, toggle any time.
- **Tilt controls** — can be switched off for button-only play.

## Scoring
Your results screen shows correct count, passes, accuracy, and your best streak, plus the full list of words you got and passed — perfect for settling arguments about that one word.

## Making your own deck
Tap **My own deck** on the home screen, give it a name, and type at least 5 words (one per line). Great for family inside jokes, class vocabulary, or roasting your friends.

## 👥 Team match mode

Flip on **Team match** in Settings. Team One and Team Two take turns playing rounds — any decks, the ready screen says whose turn it is. After each team has played **two rounds**, the higher total wins the match. A tie keeps going: the teams trade extra rounds until one pulls ahead. Scores are kept on the results screen and on the home screen while a match is live, and a match in progress survives closing the app. A round you end early from the pause menu doesn't count — finish the clock for it to score.

## 🎲 Surprise me

The **Surprise me** button next to the search bar drops you straight into a random deck. On the results screen, **Replay missed words** starts a quick round made only of the words you passed.
